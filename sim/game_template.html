What you just measured

Fault tolerant time interval (FTTI). In ISO 26262 this is the time from a fault until a hazard occurs if nothing reacts. Fault detection plus fault reaction has to fit inside it.

This system's FTTI is about 200 ms. In the study's reference hop, with the fault in the inverted part of the roll, the car lands within limits if the failed line is isolated within 180 to 200 ms. At 200 to 220 ms it lands harder than the 5 m/s limit. Later, it tips over.

Why this matters for the design. A person cannot be the safety mechanism. A typical reaction to a visual cue is about 250 ms, before any decision. So the study requires detection and isolation in 20 ms (TSR-09), done by a monitor on separate hardware, and it checks the whole chain.

Textbook safe states can be wrong. Cutting all gas, the usual reaction, lands this car on its roof. The animated fault viewer shows both side by side.
